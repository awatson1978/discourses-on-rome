Persepolis: Dispersion Check (H5) — inconclusive

Coefficient of variation of grain quantities (BAR) per regnal year, all text types pooled. The reported finding of increased dispersion around 499 BCE is NOT supported or refuted here: n is tiny and types are mixed. Honesty, not evidence.

Built 2026-11-16. All quotations in the underlying dataset are machine-verified verbatim substrings of their cited sources.
