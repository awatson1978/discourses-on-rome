In Transalpine Gaul the proconsul Gaius Sextius defeated the Salluvii and founded the colony of Aquae Sextiae, so named for the abundance of its hot and cold springs and for himself [61.1]. The proconsul Gnaeus Domitius fought with success at the town of Vindalium against the Allobroges, who had sheltered the fleeing Salluvian king Toutomotulus and ravaged the lands of the Aedui [61.2].

At home, Gaius Gracchus, after a seditious tribunate, seized the Aventine with an armed multitude; he was driven out and killed by the consul Lucius Opimius, acting under a decree of the senate, and with him perished the ex-consul Fulvius Flaccus [61.3].

The Allobroges were received in surrender [61.6]. Lucius Opimius, accused before the people by the tribune Quintus Decius on the charge that he had cast citizens into prison uncondemned, was acquitted [61.7].
