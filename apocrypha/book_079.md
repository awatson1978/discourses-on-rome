The consul Lucius Cornelius Cinna, carrying pernicious laws by violence and arms, was driven from the city by his colleague Gnaeus Octavius together with six tribunes of the plebs, and stripped of his command; then, having won over the corrupted army of Appius Claudius, he made war upon the city, summoning Gaius Marius and the other exiles from Africa [79.1].
