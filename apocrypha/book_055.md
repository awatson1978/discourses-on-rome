The tribunes of the plebs, failing to obtain the right each to exempt ten soldiers of his own choosing, ordered the consuls to be led to prison [55.2]. Decimus Junius thoroughly subdued Lusitania, as far as the Ocean, by storming its cities; and when his soldiers refused to cross the river Oblivion, he seized the standard from the bearer and carried it across himself, and so persuaded them to cross [55.7].
