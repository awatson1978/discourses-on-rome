*Era: 292–219 BCE — Third Samnite aftermath, Pyrrhic War, First Punic War, interwar years.*

Envoys of the Roman people were killed by the Senonian Gauls; for this crime war was declared upon the Gauls, and the praetor L. Caecilius, with his legions, was cut down by them [12.1]. The Tarentines plundered a Roman fleet and killed the duumvir who commanded it; and when the envoys of the senate, sent to complain of these injuries, were assaulted by them, war was declared upon them also [12.2].
