The consul Q. Pompeius subdued the Termestini in Spain [54.1]. With the same people, and with the Numantines, he made a peace which the Roman people declared void [54.2]. The lustrum was closed by the censors; the heads of citizens counted were three hundred and twenty-eight thousand four hundred and forty-two [54.3].
