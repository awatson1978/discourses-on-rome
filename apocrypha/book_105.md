When the elections were being blocked by the vetoes of the tribune of the plebs Gaius Cato, the senate changed its dress [105.1].

Marcus Cato suffered defeat in his candidacy for the praetorship, Vatinius being preferred [105.2]. And when the same Cato obstructed the law by which provinces were given to the consuls for five years — the Spains to Pompeius, Syria and the Parthian war to Crassus — he was led off to prison by the tribune of the plebs Gaius Trebonius, the author of the law [105.3].

The proconsul Aulus Gabinius brought Ptolemy back into his kingdom of Egypt, ejecting Archelaus, whom the Egyptians had adopted as their king [105.4].
