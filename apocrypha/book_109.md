Here are related the causes and the beginnings of the civil war, and the disputes about sending a successor to Gaius Caesar, who refused to dismiss his armies unless Pompeius dismissed his [109.1]. The tribune of the plebs Gaius Curio acted first against Caesar, and then for him [109.2].
