Marcus Antonius, defeated with his fleet at Actium, fled to Alexandria. [133.1] There, besieged by Caesar and in utter desperation, he killed himself, driven above all by the false rumor that Cleopatra had been slain. [133.2] Caesar brought Alexandria into his power; and Cleopatra, that she might not come into the victor's discretion, died a voluntary death. [133.3] Returning to the city, Caesar celebrated three triumphs — one over Illyricum, a second for the victory at Actium, a third over Cleopatra — an end being put to the civil wars in their twenty-second year.
