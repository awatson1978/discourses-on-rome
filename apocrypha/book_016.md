*Era: 292–219 BCE — Third Samnite aftermath, Pyrrhic War, First Punic War, interwar years.*

The origin of the Carthaginians and the beginnings of their city are set forth [16.1]. The senate, after a contested debate, decreed that aid be brought to the Mamertines against the Carthaginians and against Hiero, king of the Syracusans [16.2].
