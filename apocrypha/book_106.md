Julia, Caesar's daughter and the wife of Pompeius, died; and the people granted her the honor of burial in the Campus Martius [106.1].

Several peoples of the Gauls revolted under the leadership of Ambiorix, king of the Eburones, by whom Caesar's legates Cotta and Titurius were ambushed and cut down together with the army they commanded [106.2]. The camps of other legions also were attacked and defended with great effort; and the enemy attacking the camp which Quintus Cicero commanded among the Treveri were routed in battle by Caesar himself [106.3].

Marcus Crassus, about to make war upon the Parthians, crossed the river Euphrates, and was defeated in a battle in which his son also fell [106.4].
